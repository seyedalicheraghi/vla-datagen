# Realism backlog

What makes the drone scene look unlike real drone footage, and what to change. Agents read this file before every
round of `/realism`. The planner picks from it; the fleet-scribe marks items done, rejected or tried, with the round
id.

**Where it comes from.** It started from a static audit on 2026-10-18:

- Five agents read the code, one per area, and looked up real-world values.
- A second agent re-checked every item against the code; its corrections are applied below.
- A completeness critic added what the five missed.

**Nothing has been rendered yet.** Every item is a hypothesis until a probe shows it.

**How to read an item.**

- `impact` (1-5): how much a viewer of the video would notice it.
- `effort` (S, M, L).
- `status`: open / done (round, commit) / rejected (round, why) / tried (round, what happened).
- `values: unsourced`: the real-world numbers came from memory. The cloud machine that wrote the audit could not
  reach most websites. Before a candidate relies on such a number, the planner asks scene-researcher to confirm it
  on the PC, with a source.
- **Still names** are `<camera>_<moment>`:
  - cameras: `front`, `back`, `chase`, `nadir`;
  - moments: `ground`, `liftoff`, `climb`, `cruise_early`, `cruise_mid`, `cruise_late`, `descent`, `final`.
  - The back camera looks only about 8° down at cruise (20° tilt minus the 12° forward lean), so `back_cruise_mid`
    is the view that shows the horizon and far terrain.
- **Map changes.** In a candidate they need the cache-version suffix: `BUILD_VERSION = "<old>+<candidate id>"`, and
  likewise `TEXTURE_VERSION` and `PREPARE_VERSION`.

## Start here: the order that wastes the fewest GPU hours

### 1. Gates: measure before changing anything

These are part of `/first-run` or round r001. Several later items depend on their answers.

- `light-hdrcolor-scale-and-camera-exposure`: is HdrColor in a usable range? Check for fp16 underflow in the shadows
  and NaN. If it is wrong, every LUCID frame is wrong.
- `light-sun-sky-balance-units`: render sun-only and sky-only frames, and check that the sun-to-sky ratio is the one
  the calibration assumed. The DistantLight and DomeLight intensity units are an assumption.
- `light-dome-azimuth-check`: is the sky's bright side where the sun is? Do this before any change to the sky.
- `ground-s2-offset-date-sanity`: are the Sentinel-2 reflectances read with the right offset? They set the colour of
  all the ground.
- `light-far-clip-horizon-reach`: do the onboard cameras' 10 km far clip planes cut the land short of the horizon?
  Two probes, 10 km and 40 km. Gates the haze and horizon items. Same question as `camera-far-clip-horizon`.
- `camera-tool-determinism-settings`: render the same code twice. If repeat renders differ, A/B decisions need more
  care. The `/first-run` noise calibration does part of this.

### 2. Confirmed defects with a small fix

- `vegetation-red-oak-fall-leaf-textures`: **confirmed.** NVIDIA's Red_Oak, about 28 % of all trees, binds autumn
  leaf textures (orange-red `oakleaves1_fall*`). Switch it to the summer textures.
- `ground-crop-canopy-closed` with `vegetation-crop-canopy-mid-july`: crop fields show June-style soil between the
  rows. In mid-July the canopy is closed. The texture-cache trap that would have hidden this change is fixed (see
  below).
- `urban-roof-albedo-palette`: shingle roofs are too light. Calibrate them against the NAIP squares.
- `urban-style-hash-correlation`: roof colour is tied to roof shape through a shared random number. One-line fix.

### 3. The biggest gaps

- `light-aerial-perspective-haze`: there is no haze at all. Distant land is as crisp and saturated as near land.
  Coordinate with `ground-horizon-ring-flat-colour` so haze is not applied twice.
- `camera-aa-resample-sharpness` and `camera-temporal-aa-ghosting`: how sharp the frames are, and whether
  anti-aliasing smears or shimmers at 10 m/s. The ghosting test needs `camera-tool-probe-motion-segment` first,
  because today's probe stills hold the drone still.
- Missing everyday objects: parked cars, driveways, parking lots, fences and poles (`urban-*`).

### 4. Tools that make later rounds cheaper

- `camera-tool-probe-motion-segment`: stills with a short real flight lead-in, so temporal effects show.
- `camera-tool-hdr-resense`: save the linear render once and re-run the sensor model offline. Exposure, noise and
  colour candidates then need no GPU at all.
- `camera-tool-map-cache-isolation`: map caches per candidate, keyed by the world/ code itself.
- `camera-tool-fullres-timing-defaults`: a full-resolution preset for camera items, a fixed-exposure preset for
  material A/B, and timings in probe.json.

### Already done (outside the loop)

- **Crop-row texture cache trap.** `world/materials.py` generated the crop texture under the unversioned name
  `crop_rows_*.png`, so a changed crop texture would silently reuse the old file. It now carries `TEXTURE_VERSION`
  in its name, like the facade and roof textures. `BUILD_VERSION` is now 6. (Raised by `ground-crop-canopy-closed`
  and `vegetation-crop-canopy-mid-july`.)

## Overlaps to keep in mind

- **Haze:** `light-aerial-perspective-haze`, `ground-horizon-ring-flat-colour`, `light-horizon-seam-and-flat-ring`,
  `camera-far-clip-horizon`. Do one haze model, then check the horizon.
- **Exposure:** `light-ae-metering-includes-sky`, `camera-tone-ae-clip`, `light-ae-dynamics-and-limits`. All of them
  change the sensor side; test them offline once `camera-tool-hdr-resense` exists.
- **Crops:** `ground-crop-canopy-closed`, `vegetation-crop-canopy-mid-july`, `ground-crop-corn-vs-soy`,
  `ground-crop-tile-repeat-3m`, `ground-crop-no-height-field-edges`. Do canopy closure first; it is the cheapest and
  most visible.
- **Lawn colour:** `ground-patch-quilt`, `vegetation-lawn-hue-dry-patches-s2-scene`,
  `vegetation-lawn-texture-scale-and-mowing-stripes`. Lawn calibration already uses the greenest 30 % on purpose:
  WorldCover "lawn" pixels contain roofs and streets. Do not switch back to the median.

## Lighting, sky, atmosphere and exposure

### light-hdrcolor-scale-and-camera-exposure: Check the HdrColor scale before trusting the sensor sim
`impact 3 · effort S · status: open · values: sourced`
- **Now:** rtx_look.py:103 sets `"/rtx/post/histogram/enabled": False`; the docstring (rtx_look.py:21-24) says "no exposure key is set"; no exposure schema is authored on the onboard camera prims (quadcopter_pbr.py:613-620). quadcopter_pbr.py:679 assumes HdrColor is in scene light units (`grey = 0.18 * reference_illuminance / pi`); the log text at :701-703 and the comment at :696 ("differ in scale by ~1000x") admit it is not. sensor_sim.py:220 maps NaN to 0 and +inf to 65504; the NaN fallback at quadcopter_pbr.py:689 works.
- **Real world:** Third-party measurement on Isaac Sim 6.1.0-rc.26 / Kit 110, not 5.1: HdrColor is fp16 x4, linear in colour x intensity with slope about 2.8e-4, the scale lives in the camera exposure schema, at intensity 1 the ramp sits in the fp16 subnormal range (smallest normal 2^-14 = 6.1e-5), and under RealTimePathTracing it read all NaN: https://github.com/reza-shahriari/isaac-thermal-camera-simulation/blob/15015cd24cdabfc103d9c2afd0a42b28106bf34d/docs/decisions/0014-isaac-sim-6-1-findings.md . Isaac Lab (newer main) neutralises exposure per camera (OmniRtxCameraAutoExposureAPI_1 + OmniRtxCameraExposureAPI_1, exposure 0.0, fStop 1.0, iso 0.0, responsivity 1.0, time 1.0, autoExposure:enabled False): https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab/blob/9b430f0d5ddf1c5f801072d6c5600aa6e879d2b3/source/isaaclab_ppisp/isaaclab_ppisp/rtx_camera_overrides.py . Whether Kit 107 has these schemas is unverified.
- **Change:** First step is a measurement, no scene change: on the first probe frame of each onboard camera print min / p1 / median / p99 / max of HdrColor G, the NaN count and median / 18 % grey (the ratio already logged at quadcopter_pbr.py:701), in real-time and in `--pathtracing` (the third-party table has no PathTracing result). Also halve dome_intensity and expect half the HdrColor. If median < 1e-3 or p1 < 6.1e-5: check prim.GetAppliedSchemas() / get_setting (a harmless no-op if the names are absent), author the neutral exposure schema on the `{body}/{name}_cam` prims, and add a camera EV offset so the median lies in 0.05-1. The old "avoid realtime2" advice is moot: quadcopter_pbr.py:547-551 selects only pathtracing or realtime.
- **Check:** front_ground, back_ground, back_cruise_mid in both render modes (log lines, not pictures). Acceptance: NaN count 0; G median 0.05-1; p1 >= 6.1e-5; halving test within 5 % of 0.5 (proposed tolerance). After any fix, mean_luma, clip_lo and noise_sigma of front_ground and back_cruise_mid stay within render noise, and chase_cruise_mid (Isaac's tone-mapped rgb) does not change.
- **Cost:** No map rebuild, no version bump. Two probe renders (real-time, path-traced) at --resolution_scale 0.5. Run this first: light-sun-sky-balance-units, light-aerial-perspective-haze and light-rt-vs-pt-shade-fill all read HdrColor. Do not combine the schema with rtx_look `exposure_ev`. No owner decision.

### light-far-clip-horizon-reach: Does the 10 km camera far clip cut the land before the horizon?
`impact 4 · effort S · status: open · values: unsourced`
- **Now:** The front and back LUCID cameras use `clipping_range=(0.01, 10000.0)` (quadcopter_pbr.py:619); chase and nadir use 20000 (:624, :632). The map is `--far 6000` around the route centre (world/build.py:76) plus a flat-colour ring out to `--horizon 25000` (build.py:77; ring at :385-398 and :601-611). If RTX cuts primary rays at the far clip (usual, not verified), land ends at 10 km and the ring is visible only from about 6-7 km to 10 km, 3-4 km wide.
- **Real world:** Geometry only: from 40 m the horizon is 22.6 km away and 0.203 deg below horizontal; a land edge at the 10 km clip sits atan(40/10000) = 0.23 deg below horizontal, about 4-5 px at 19-20 px/deg. Earth curvature inside 10 km changes that by under 1 px. (unsourced: have scene-researcher confirm before relying on it)
- **Change:** First step is a measurement: `--probe cruise_mid --cameras front back` twice, far clip 10000 and 40000 at quadcopter_pbr.py:619, everything else equal. If the land stops short with 10000 and reaches the horizon with 40000, set 40000 for the onboard cameras (also look at front_ground and front_final for near-field artefacts; the near clip stays 0.01 m). If both renders match, RTX does not cut at the clip: drop the clip caveats from the haze and horizon items.
- **Check:** back_cruise_mid (10000 vs 40000) and front_cruise_early (horizon about 4 % from the top). Acceptance: the gap between the last land row and the horizontal (0 deg) row, where the dome horizon sits, is at most 1-2 px with 40000 (ring edge about 0.09 deg below horizontal) against about 4-5 px with 10000 if the clip cuts; haze_contrast differs between the two renders.
- **Cost:** No map rebuild, no version bump; two probes at full resolution (at 0.5 scale the 4-5 px gap shrinks to 2 px). Gates light-aerial-perspective-haze (depth beyond the clip reads as sky, so haze would skip the distance that needs it) and light-horizon-seam-and-flat-ring (extending the ring to 40 km is useless unless the clip passes the ring edge, up to about 26 km from the drone). New item from the audit corrections. No owner decision.

### light-sun-sky-balance-units: Measure the sun-to-sky light balance in RTX
`impact 4 · effort S · status: open · values: partly sourced`
- **Now:** hdr.py:35-38 treats DistantLight `intensity` as a normalised irradiance and DomeLight `intensity` as a multiplier of the HDR radiance (scene.py:535 dome, :539 sun, :540 colour, :541 angle). Cached numbers (earlier cloud run at sun 48.8 deg, not the straight route): dome 692.46, sun 4508.34, sun_color [1.0, 0.9419, 0.8630], reference_illuminance 4090.31. Derived: sun:sky horizontal = 4.888 x sin(el) = 4.30 at 61.5 deg, sky 18.9 %, sunlit:shade 5.3:1; at 61.5 deg expect dome about 611, sun about 3980. rtx_look.py:21-24 still quotes the old 900 / 3800. Nothing measures what RTX does with the two numbers.
- **Real world:** Kit 110 docs, not verified for Isaac Sim 5.1 (Kit 107): DistantLight intensity is "Base luminance in nits for an unnormalized light" (fallback 50000), photometric illuminance is lux on a surface facing the sun (distant) or facing up (dome): https://github.com/NVIDIA-Omniverse/ovrtx/blob/main/docs/lights/common_attributes.rst and https://github.com/NVIDIA-Omniverse/ovrtx/blob/main/docs/lights/physical_lighting.rst . If the sun is read as nits of a 0.53 deg disc it is about 1.5e4 times weaker, so do not assume the error is within 3x. Target sun share 80-90 % (sunlit:shade 6:1 to 10:1) is unsourced; weak hint only: https://github.com/Tuan6b/Lumarit/blob/ff137f7db65c148d552d04daf509ec4f10b16cba/shaders/lib/settings.glsl ("about 6:1").
- **Change:** First step is a measurement: add `--light_debug` that, at the first probe moment, renders the same view three times with (a) Sun intensity 0, (b) Sky intensity 0, (c) both on (intensity 0, not visibility: an invisible dome also drops the background) and reads HdrColor luminance (not RGB, sun_color is not neutral) on a flat open patch of lawn or paver. Predicted sun-only / sky-only = 4.30 at 61.5 deg; log measured / predicted. If outside 0.85-1.15, multiply sun_intensity at prepare.py:80 by predicted / measured. Only then pick a target sunlit:shade (6-8:1 means sun_intensity x1.2-1.5 at constant total) from the owner's reference footage. Log the ratio next to the "mean scene radiance" line (quadcopter_pbr.py:701).
- **Check:** front_ground and back_ground (patio and lawn in view), log lines for the ratio. Acceptance: measured / predicted within 0.85-1.15. After a rescale, contrast and clip_lo of front_ground rise with a larger sun share while clip_hi stays under 2 %, and the blind judges do not prefer the old shadows.
- **Cost:** No map rebuild; PREPARE_VERSION (prepare.py:35) becomes "3+light-sun-sky-balance-units" if a factor is applied. That suffix only changes the manifest key (`_key`, prepare.py:40-44): prepare_sky returns the cached `<cache>/sky/<sky>_nosun_sun<el>.json` without looking at the version (prepare.py:56-64), so the candidate must also put the version into those cache file names or the factor never reaches a render. Three frames at one pose. Run after light-hdrcolor-scale-and-camera-exposure (the ratios need linear HdrColor); light-sky-hdr-wrong-sun-elevation and light-rt-vs-pt-shade-fill must re-check or reuse it. Owner decides the target sunlit:shade ratio. Changes shadow contrast of every frame; exposure_us in camera_info shifts.

### light-ae-metering-includes-sky: Auto exposure meters the sky as well as the ground
`impact 3 · effort S · status: open · values: partly sourced`
- **Now:** sensor_sim.py:56 `ae_target = 0.22`; `_meter()` (sensor_sim.py:236) is a plain mean of the clipped raw signal over every pixel, sky included. scripts/drone_scene/README.md:59-61 says "A bright sky therefore clips", probably not true here: sky is about 2-3x a sunlit lawn (about 0.40 vs 0.17, verifier's estimate), so near 0.4-0.6 at target 0.22, below clipping. The real effect is the ground getting darker as sky area grows. Back camera axis is 20 - 12 = 8 deg below horizontal at cruise (quadcopter_pbr.py:161, flight.py:221-222): sky about 40 % of rows (hover about 24 %, front at cruise about 4-5 %).
- **Real world:** LUCID cameras run their own auto exposure (ExposureAuto Continuous, ExposureAutoDamping, ExposureAutoLowerLimit/UpperLimit, TargetBrightness); a driver comment says "70 is the optimal target brightness value for outdoor usage, as stated in the camera's manual" (70/255 = 0.27, linear or gamma-coded unknown; the repo's 0.22 linear is sRGB 128/255): https://github.com/tidewise/drivers-orogen-camera_lucid/blob/2ecebdd4e2944d4f1da06dfc3e6c2498dd9466b0/tasks/Task.cpp . The metered area (whole frame or ROI) and consumer-drone metering are unsourced: do not retune ae_target from the 70 alone.
- **Change:** First step is a measurement: ground mean luma (lowest 40 % of rows) at cruise_mid for `--camera_pitch 10`, `20`, `30` (sky 20-50 %); expect more than 0.3 stop spread today. Also log clip_hi on back and front at cruise_mid (this replaces the dropped light-isp-hard-clip-no-rolloff). If the spread is real: add `ae_sky_weight` / ae ROI to SensorSettings (sensor_sim.py:52-62) and `--ae_target`, `--ae_sky_weight` flags (both SensorSettings constructions, quadcopter_pbr.py:659 and :801); weight sky pixels 0.25 in `_meter()` using the geometric horizon row from pose and K (not a "2.5x median" mask, which also hits white roofs); log sky fraction, ground mean luma, saturated fraction.
- **Check:** back_cruise_mid at --camera_pitch 10 / 20 / 30, front_cruise_mid as control (almost no sky). Acceptance: mean_luma of a crop of the lowest 40 % of rows varies by less than 0.3 stop across the three pitches; clip_hi under about 2 % on front_cruise_mid and not raised on back_cruise_mid beyond the sky area.
- **Cost:** No map rebuild, no version bump. Three half-resolution probes. Changes the brightness of every video. Re-run after light-aerial-perspective-haze (haze flattens contrast and the AE lifts it) and light-sky-hdr-wrong-sun-elevation. Owner decides the metering model from reference footage; the real LUCID metering area is unknown.

### light-ae-dynamics-and-limits: Auto exposure reacts in 0.09 s with no limits
`impact 2 · effort S · status: open · values: partly sourced`

- **Real world:** canopies shimmer and roll, crops show travelling waves of leaf flips, and ponds ripple. Wind
  speeds for Ames in July are unsourced.
- **Change:**
  - Add `wind.py`: one global wind vector, also passed to `StraightFlight.wind` so the drone's lean agrees, plus a
    travelling gust field.
  - Each captured frame, tilt the tree and grass instances within about 400 m of the drone by a small, height-scaled

- **Check:** needs `camera-tool-probe-motion-segment`; single stills cannot show motion. Use frame-to-frame
  differences on foliage crops, and the judges on short clips.
- **Cost:** runtime only. Rewriting thousands of instance transforms each frame costs render time: measure it.

### camera-takeoff-landing-no-rotor-wash: grass and leaves ignore the rotors
`impact 3 · effort M · status: open · values: unsourced`
- **Now:** the drone is teleported along the flight (`place()`). The backyard grass, the leaves and the landing
  meadow do not react while it hovers 0.2-4 m above them (`flight.py` final descent at 0.5 m/s).
- **Real world:** rotor downwash (momentum theory: v = sqrt(T / (2 rho A)), T = 11.8 N) bends grass and blows
  leaves within a metre or two. The LMF2 rotor disc area must be taken from its USD.
- **Change:** add `rotor_wash.py`, called after `place()`.
  - Tilt grass clumps away from the drone by `theta_max * exp(-(d / (0.8 + 0.6 h))^2)`, faded out above 6 m, plus a
    small flutter.
  - Push leaves outward.
- **Check:** `front_liftoff`, `front_final` (still shows the bend); motion clips show the flutter.
- **Cost:** runtime only. Coordinate with `vegetation-backyard-autumn-leaves-and-planting`.

### camera-pinhole-no-defocus-uniform-lens-blur: no depth of field, same blur everywhere
`impact 2 · effort S · status: open · values: computed`
- **Now:** the onboard `PinholeCameraCfg` has no `f_stop` or `focus_distance` (`quadcopter_pbr.py`, onboard camera
  spawn), so focus is perfect at every distance. `Rig.hyperfocal_m()` (`lucid.py:226`) is used only by a test.
  `blur_px = 0.5` (`lucid.py:80`) is the same at the centre and the corners.
- **Real world:** at 4 mm f/2.8 with a 2 px circle of confusion the hyperfocal distance is about 0.83 m (computed).
  It matters only at take-off and landing, where ground within a metre is soft. Real lenses are softer in the
  corners.
- **Change:**
  - Give the onboard cameras `f_stop = 2.8` and a `--focus_m` flag (default hyperfocal).
  - Make the blur field-dependent: `sigma(r) = sigma0 + (sigma_corner - sigma0) r^2`.
- **Check:** `front_ground`, `front_final`; the `sharpness` of corner crops against centre crops.
- **Cost:** small; coordinate with `camera-aa-resample-sharpness`. The real-time DoF key is unverified for Kit 107.

### camera-record-asset-streaming-popin: the video never waits for textures
`impact 3 · effort S · status: open · values: n/a`
- **Now:** `SimulationManager.assets_loading()` is polled only before the first frame and, in probe mode, at each

- **Real world:** a real camera never shows pop-in.
- **Change:** first measure: log `assets_loading()` and the wall time of every captured frame into the per-frame

- **Check:** a 60 s `/record` segment: count the frames with assets still loading, and look at frame-to-frame jumps.

### ground-no-lot-scale-colour-from-aerial-imagery: ground and roof colour has no detail finer than 10 m
`impact 4 · effort L · status: open · values: partly sourced`
- **Now:**
  - Ground colour is the class texture times one Sentinel-2 median tint per class, times a brightness factor per
    25 m patch (`build.py:207-282`). The finest colour source is Sentinel-2 at 10 m.
  - Roof and wall styles are a hash (`buildings.py:61-95`).
  - `realism/reference.py` already reads NAIP at 0.6 m for comparison.
- **Real world:** NAIP is about 17x finer per axis than Sentinel-2. Lots, driveways, garden beds, bare patches,
  pools and individual roofs are all in it.
- **Change:**
  - Add `data.naip()` reusing `reference.cut()` and `signed()`. Keep Sentinel-2 as the colour truth and use NAIP
    only for structure: the ratio of its 0.6 m luma to its own 10 m blur, clamped to 0.5-1.8, multiplied into the
    face tint.
  - Sample each roof's colour from NAIP.
  - The comparison then uses a different NAIP year, or keeps some squares back as a held-out test.
- **Check:** `nadir_cruise_mid` against NAIP (`colour_dE`, `ssim_coarse`), `front_cruise_mid`.
- **Cost:**
  - Map rebuild (`BUILD_VERSION`, `TEXTURE_VERSION`).
  - NAIP brightness values are not calibrated and contain the shadows of its own flight, hence the ratio-only use.
  - It makes the NAIP comparison partly circular: decide on a hold-out first.
